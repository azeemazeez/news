The Nuus

Serving you bite-sized news, every day.

The Nuus is built to be read, not to read you. This policy covers the website (thenuus.com) and The Nuus iOS app.

What we collect. The iOS app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs — it simply fetches the day's stories from our server. The website is the same, with one exception: if you choose to subscribe to the daily email digest, we store the email address you provide.

How we use it. A subscribed email address is used for one thing only: sending you the daily digest. We never sell, share, or use it for anything else. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes your address.

Third-party links. Stories link out to the original publishers. Once you leave The Nuus, those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Questions? Contact us at hi@thenuus.com.
